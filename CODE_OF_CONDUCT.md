# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good faith, provide constructive feedback, and respect different experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy. Report concerns privately to the project maintainers until a dedicated contact address is published.
